ARCHITECTURE CHANGE:
- Lexing should be a distinct pass from parsing, which has several advantages:
1. Arbitrary length lookahead when parsing with almost no cost.
2. Better separation between lexing errors and parsing errors.
3. Opportunities to pipeline the lexer and parser for increased performance.
